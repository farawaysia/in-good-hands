Your data and privacy

Your journal is yours alone. This is how we handle what you write, in line with Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

Only you can read it
Your entries and your sleep and mood ratings are visible only to you. They are never shown to other family members on the plan, to your funeral director, or to our counselling partners, unless you ask us to share something.
Used only to run your journal
We collect your ratings and your words so we can show them back to you, and for nothing else. We don’t use them for advertising and we never sell them.
Your consent comes first
Keeping a journal is optional. We ask for your consent before you start, and you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing doesn’t affect the rest of your plan.
You stay in control
Edit or delete any entry whenever you like. You can also ask us for a copy of your journal, or ask us to correct or erase it.
We keep it protected
We use reasonable security arrangements to guard your journal against unauthorised access. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).
Not kept longer than needed
When you delete an entry, it is removed from your journal. If you close your account, we delete your journal too.
Questions or requests
To ask for a copy of your journal, or to correct, erase or withdraw consent, write to our Data Protection Officer: [DPO CONTACT EMAIL]
