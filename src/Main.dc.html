My story

Why I'm Building This

At 19, I lost my father suddenly to a heart attack.

Before the funeral, I ended up in charge, with no idea what to do. The hospital handed me a list of 22 funeral directors: only names, websites and phone numbers. In my confusion I picked one at random. Only years later did I find out we had paid almost double the market price.

After the funeral, the paperwork began: closing his bank and insurance accounts one by one, while my family was still crumbling in grief.

Then grief hit me. Brain fog made daily life a struggle, and it took me two years and 33 counselling sessions to find my footing again.

My experience isn't unique. That's why I'm building In Good Hands: so the next family has help at every step, and no one has to carry it alone.

— Felicia, founder

What is In Good Hands?

A website that helps families in Singapore through a loss: choosing a funeral provider, sharing the paperwork, and looking after yourself through grief.

Is it free?

The Guide, provider matching, the checklist and the journal are free. From September 2027, families who want hands-on help closing a loved one's accounts will be able to pay for it.

Is it an AI or a real person?

Both. An AI Guide explains what to do and asks a few gentle questions. At every step there's a real person to call: our care team, and partner counsellors and lawyers.

How does provider matching work?

Tell the Guide about your beliefs, budget and needs, and it suggests funeral providers that fit, with prices, what's included and what to watch for. We aim to stay independent and on the family's side.

Can my whole family use it?

Yes. Family members join one shared plan, split the checklist between them and see what's done, so no one carries it alone.

What does account closure cover?

The dashboard lists the accounts to close, such as banks, insurance and phone lines, and tracks each one. Hands-on help, where we handle the paperwork with you, is planned from September 2027.

Who can read my journal?

Only you. Your journal and mood check-ins aren't shared with the rest of your family.

How do you protect my family's information?

It comes first. We're building our document and account features with cybersecurity and Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) in mind, and file sharing stays off until it can be secured properly.

Is this legal or financial advice?

No. The Guide gives general information. For matters like wills or an estate, our partner lawyers can help.
