How it works Try the concierge

The hardest week of your life shouldn't also be the most confusing one.

  • Grief comes first, and it doesn't wait. It's tiring in body & mind — not ending when the funeral does.
  • Nobody plans for this. There's no common playbook — nobody likes to talk about it, and nothing tells you what to do next, or what's normal.
  • Big decisions, in a matter of hours. The closest relative has no choice but to decide quickly, while still in shock — exactly the conditions where costly mistakes happen.

Not just our story

Singapore's own research says the same thing

  • Confidence rises to 59% among people who've actually done it before — most of us are just guessing the first time.

  • Most said they simply ran out of time, or went with whoever a friend or relative recommended.

  • The most common surprise: add-ons agreed on later, during the wake itself, that weren't in the original package.

  • Most providers don't publish prices at all — you usually have to already be in contact with one to find out what anything costs. That's the gap we're built to close.

Source: Competition & Consumer Commission of Singapore, Funeral Services Industry Market Study, 17 Nov 2023

A person, on your side of the table

  • Reads contracts line by line and flags hidden costs and unrealistic promises
  • Negotiates for you, with clear rates and no hidden mark-ups
  • Helps family members who disagree find common ground
  • Sits with you in person, if that's what would help

Never have to explain twice.

Note

The Guide is an AI. It is not a lawyer, a doctor or a funeral director, and it won't pretend to be one. When something needs an expert, it says so and brings in a person.

We don't sell funeral packages. We represent families, not providers, and we publish our fees so you can see exactly what you're paying for. Our first families get concierge help free, in exchange for honest feedback — and it's lower for CHAS cardholders too.
